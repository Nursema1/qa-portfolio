# RISK MATRIX — Login & Checkout

Purpose:
Identify high-risk areas to prioritize testing.

| Feature | Risk Level | Reason |
|---|---|---|
Login Authentication | High | Critical entry point to system |
Login Authorization | High | Unauthorized access risk |
Token Handling | High | Expired token misuse possible |
Checkout Payment | High | Financial transaction |
Card Validation | Medium | Input validation errors possible |
Error Messaging | Medium | Information disclosure risk |
